Use the internet's good parts. Skip the doomscroll.

Zuckless removes the addictive bits of your favorite sites while leaving the useful bits alone. Buy that couch on Marketplace, message your friends, watch your videos, all without getting pulled into feeds, comment wars, and reply threads.

WHAT IT BLOCKS

🙈 Facebook Social Feed
The feed is gone everywhere, and facebook.com drops you straight into Marketplace instead of the scroll. Marketplace and Messages work exactly like normal.

💬 Facebook Comments (off by default)
Keeping the feed? Flip this on to hide comments, the comment box, and "View more comments" links on posts. Your feed, minus the arguments.

📸 Instagram Comments
Comment threads and the comment box disappear. Photos, carousels, reels, and stories stay.

▶️ YouTube Comments
The comment section under videos and Shorts is gone. Just watch the video.

🐦 Twitter/X Replies
Open a post and see the post itself, with its quote tweet, photos, and video. Not the reply brawl underneath it.

YOU'RE IN CONTROL

Every blocker has its own on/off switch in the popup. Flips apply instantly to open tabs, no reload needed. Want comments back for one video? Two clicks, and two clicks to hide them again.

PRIVACY FIRST

Zuckless collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no data ever leaves your browser. Your toggle settings sync through your own Chrome profile. The only permissions it requests are the ones the features need.

GENTLE BY DESIGN

Zuckless hides content with CSS instead of ripping pages apart, so the sites keep working exactly as designed. No broken layouts, no interference with the pages you keep.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE

The full source code is public at github.com/scottylad007/zuckless, MIT licensed. See exactly what runs in your browser.
